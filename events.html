BEFORE YOU BOOK

Questions about your event

Can I ask about a date before choosing a venue?

Yes. Enter the town or area and tell us the venue is still to be confirmed.

Can I send a list of songs?

Include a few favourites in your enquiry. You can share a longer list when you discuss the booking with JST.

What should I include when asking for a quote?

Send the date, venue, start and finish times, and a rough guest count. Mention any extra details, such as a first dance or a surprise entrance.

When is my date confirmed?

Once JST replies and agrees the booking with you.
