About JST.

We’re a new mobile disco business for wedding receptions, birthdays and private parties.

Planning your night

Start with your date and venue, then tell us what you’d like the night to sound like. If there’s a first dance, a favourite era or a song that always gets your family up, let us know.

It’s also worth mentioning anything we should know about the room, such as stairs, access times or a sound limiter.

Where we work

We’re based in West Norfolk. If your event is in King’s Lynn, Hunstanton or a nearby village, send us the venue postcode so we can check the location.
